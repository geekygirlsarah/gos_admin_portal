{# ── Why Teams Choose Momentum / Core Pillars ── #}
Why Teams Switch

From Spreadsheets to a Real Team Operating System

Most robotics teams run out of duct-taped systems: spreadsheets for dues, paper rosters, and frantic emails. Momentum replaces the chaos with purpose-built workflows.

Build Season is Chaotic Enough

Admin overhead shouldn't eat your weekends. Momentum handles operational plumbing so mentors can spend their time in the shop coaching students.

Parents as Partners, Not the Unpaid Finance Dept

Automated monthly balance statements, sliding-scale financial aid, and payment recording eliminate constant “how much do I owe?” emails.

Attendance Hours are Real Data, Not Vibes

Kiosk and RFID check-ins, digital sign-outs, and visual hours charts deliver the exact verified data mentors need for lettering and awards.

Orders & Procurement Deserve a System

Student parts request pool → grouped vendor orders (AndyMark, McMaster, REV) → shipping and delivery tracking. No more ghost orders.

Mentor Coverage: Visible, Not Tribal

Outreach shift caps, mentor sign-ups with real capacity math, and phone-first event check-ins ensure no demo or workshop is left understaffed.

Secure & Multi-Tenant Isolation

Each organization operates in its own isolated workspace with custom subdomains, granular role permissions, encrypted records, and immutable audit logs.

{# ── Feature Deep-Dive Grid ── #}
Platform Capabilities

Comprehensive Team Management Modules

Engineered around the real operational lifecycle of competitive robotics programs — whether you have 15 students or 150.

{# Feature 1: Student & Parent Applications #}
Public Application Wizard

Multi-step, resumable public application workflow (/apply/) with email OTP authentication, guardian contact collection, and demographic tracking.

Key highlight: Staff review pipeline with direct applicant messaging and automated conversion to student and financial records.

{# Feature 2: Attendance Kiosk #}
Kiosk Attendance & Hours Dashboard

Fast tablet and RFID check-in/out, digital sign-out waivers with guardian signatures, daily presence tracking, and open session monitoring.

Key highlight: Visual student hours dashboard with weekly averages, date/day-of-week filters, and exportable PNG charts for varsity lettering.

{# Feature 3: Finance & Sliding Scale #}
Fees, Balance Sheets & Sliding Scale

Per-program fee structures and custom student assignments with itemized balance sheets, payment tracking, and automated monthly balance statements.

Key highlight: Integrated sliding-scale financial aid questionnaire with household size/AGI discounts and structured lead mentor approval.

{# Feature 4: Order Requests & Vendors #}
Parts Procurement & Vendor Orders

Students and mentors submit parts requests from robotics suppliers (AndyMark, McMaster-Carr, REV Robotics, WestCoast Products, and more).

Key highlight: Group unassigned items into vendor purchase orders, log carrier tracking numbers, and export budget CSV/XLSX spreadsheets.

{# Feature 5: Outreach & Travel #}
Outreach Shifts & Competition Travel

Coordinate community demonstration events with champion/helper caps, mentor sign-up requirements, and phone-friendly check-in workflows.

Key highlight: Multi-day travel trips with departure options, student signups, parent approvals, and chaperone roster management.

{# Feature 6: Communications & Calendar #}
Communications & Calendar Feeds

Organization-branded responsive email templates, rich visual editor with image embedding, and automated weekly digests tailored per role.

Key highlight: Auto-synced outreach and travel calendar feeds (ICS) with personal tokenized subscriptions for Google Calendar and Apple Calendar.

{# Feature 7: Badges & Skill Trees #}
Skill Badges & Progression

Define safety test requirements, technical milestones, machine tool sign-offs, and leadership badges for your robotics students.

Key highlight: Mentors verify prerequisites and sign off directly in the portal, giving students a visual progression map.

{# Feature 8: Dynamic Permissions & Role Management #}
Granular Role Permissions

Fine-grained, per-section read and write permission toggles for Lead Mentors, Mentors, Student Leaders, Students, and Parents.

Key highlight: Keep financial and emergency data securely scoped while delegating kiosk or order management to student leads.

{# Feature 9: Audit Logging & Data Security #}
Integrity & Immutable Audit Logs

Immutable audit trail capturing model changes, deletions, and administrative actions with full snapshot diffs and user attribution.

Key highlight: Field-level encryption for sensitive health, clearance, and financial records with complete tenant data isolation.
