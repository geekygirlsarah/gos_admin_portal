Permission Rules & Security Guardrails
  • Lead Mentors always retain full administrative access across all sections.
  • None (No Access): The user role cannot view or interact with this section.
  • Read (View Only): Allows viewing records, logs, and associated details within the section.
  • R / W (Read & Write): Allows creating, updating, and removing records within the section.
  • Data Privacy: Parents and Students are strictly restricted to their own linked records even if global write permissions are enabled.

Income Guideline Formula & Parameters

These values determine the suggested tuition discount percentage based on household size and AGI.

Formula:
fed_base = base_amount + household_size * additional_member_amount
low_boundary = fed_base * low_multiplier
high_boundary = fed_base * high_multiplier
discount_percent = 100 - ((AGI - low_boundary) / (high_boundary - low_boundary) * 100)
