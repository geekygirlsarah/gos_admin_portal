Income Guideline Settings

These numbers are used to calculate the suggested sliding scale discount from a family's household size and adjusted gross income (AGI), based on federal poverty guidelines: 

fed_base = base_amount + household_size * additional_member_amount
low_boundary = fed_base * low_multiplier
high_boundary = fed_base * high_multiplier
discount_percent = 100 - ((AGI - low_boundary) / (high_boundary - low_boundary) * 100)
