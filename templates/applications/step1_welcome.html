Who can apply?

This application lets you join a Girls of Steel Robotics program. Depending on your situation, you can apply as:

  • Students — Apply for yourself to join one of our upcoming programs for girls in grades K–12.
  • Parents / Guardians — Apply on behalf of one of your children.
  • Mentors / Volunteers — Apply to volunteer with our teams and support our mission. (Jump straight to the application below.)
