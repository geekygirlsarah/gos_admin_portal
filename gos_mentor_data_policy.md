# Girls of Steel Mentor Data Access and Safeguarding Agreement

**Effective Date: ** August 1, 2026  
**Applies To:** All mentors, volunteer mentors, lead mentors, and other approved adults with access to program records or participant information

This agreement explains how mentors must handle student, parent/guardian, alumni, and program data accessed through Girls of Steel systems or through any other approved program process.

**By accepting this agreement, I understand that I am being given access to information for the limited purpose of supporting Girls of Steel programs and participants. I agree to protect that information and use it responsibly.**

## 1. Purpose of access

I may access program information only as needed to carry out my authorized role, including activities such as:

- mentoring students
- supervising meetings, events, travel, or outreach
- communicating with participants and families for program operations
- supporting safety and emergency response
- performing approved administrative tasks

I will access only the information reasonably necessary for those purposes.

## 2. Covered information

This agreement applies to any nonpublic program information I access, including but not limited to:

- student names and contact information
- parent/guardian names and contact information
- addresses
- attendance records
- schedule and roster information
- school affiliations
- medical, allergy, emergency, or safety information
- application information
- financial or fee-related information
- internal notes, reports, or operational records
- any information about minors, families, mentors, or alumni not intended for public distribution

## 3. Permitted use

I agree that I will use covered information only for legitimate Girls of Steel purposes.

I will not use program data:

- for personal reasons
- for unrelated school, work, research, or volunteer activities
- for recruiting outside organizations, clubs, or businesses
- for personal networking or social contact unrelated to the program
- for curiosity or browsing
- in any way not authorized by Girls of Steel leadership

## 4. Minimum necessary access

I understand that access to information is based on role and need.

I agree that I will:

- access only the records needed for my assigned responsibilities
- avoid reviewing records for students, families, or programs I do not support
- not seek broader access unless there is a clear program need and it is approved

## 5. Confidentiality and nondisclosure

I will not share covered information with anyone except authorized Girls of Steel personnel who need it for program purposes.

I will not:

- share rosters, contact lists, attendance, or student information with unauthorized people
- discuss private student or family information in public or casual settings
- post private information in chat groups, social media, or collaboration tools unless specifically approved and appropriate
- disclose information to other students, parents, alumni, or mentors unless they are authorized to receive it

If I am unsure whether information may be shared, I will treat it as confidential and ask a lead mentor or administrator.

## 6. Storage and copying restrictions

I will use only approved systems, accounts, and processes to access or handle covered information.

Unless explicitly authorized, I will not:

- download and keep local copies of rosters or records
- maintain personal spreadsheets or databases of participant information
- save student or parent data in my personal contacts list
- copy information into personal notes, notebooks, or files beyond what is strictly necessary
- print records unless needed for an approved program purpose

If printing or temporary offline access is necessary for an event or emergency purpose, I will protect the information, use the minimum necessary, and destroy or delete it promptly when no longer needed.

## 7. Devices and account security

I agree to protect access to program systems and data by:

- using strong passwords
- not sharing my login with anyone
- using only my own approved account
- locking my devices when not in use
- keeping software and devices reasonably up to date
- avoiding access on shared or public devices unless absolutely necessary and done securely

I will not let students, family members, or other unauthorized individuals use my account or view information through my session.

## 8. Communications

When communicating with students or families, I will use approved channels whenever possible.

I will be careful not to include unnecessary personal information in:

- email
- text messages
- group messages
- shared documents
- calendar invites
- presentation materials

I will not send spreadsheets, exported reports, or sensitive student information through informal channels unless specifically authorized for a valid operational need.

## 9. Sensitive and emergency information

Some information, including medical, allergy, emergency, behavioral, disciplinary, financial, and other sensitive information, requires extra care.

I will:

- access such information only when needed for safety, supervision, or another approved reason
- share it only with authorized personnel who need it
- take special care during events, travel, and emergency situations
- avoid retaining sensitive information after the need has passed

## 10. System access logging and review

I understand that use of Girls of Steel systems may be logged, including account access, viewing of records, changes to records, messages, exports, and other administrative actions. These logs may be reviewed by authorized program leadership for security, safety, auditing, troubleshooting, and policy compliance.

## 11. Reporting incidents

I will immediately report any actual or suspected data issue to Girls of Steel leadership, including:

- lost or stolen devices containing program data
- information sent to the wrong person
- unauthorized access or account sharing
- screenshots, exports, or files shared improperly
- accidental disclosure in conversation, email, or messaging
- any situation where student or family information may have been exposed

I understand that prompt reporting is required even if the exposure was accidental or seems minor.

## 12. Retention and deletion

I will not keep covered information longer than necessary.

When information is no longer needed, or when my role ends, I will:

- delete local copies of program data
- destroy printed records securely
- remove program data from personal files and storage locations
- return any program-owned materials containing information

If asked, I will confirm that I have deleted or returned covered information.

## 13. End of role or access

When I leave the program, change roles, or no longer need access, I understand that my access may be reduced or removed.

At that time, I must stop using program data and must not retain, reuse, or share information obtained through my role.

## 14. Compliance with university and program policies

I understand that this agreement is a plain-language summary of data-handling expectations for Girls of Steel mentors.

I also agree to comply with:

- applicable Girls of Steel policies and procedures
- applicable Carnegie Mellon University policies, standards, guidelines, and requirements related to data stewardship, data access, privacy, information security, youth protection, and record handling
- Carnegie Mellon University guidance regarding access to institutional data, **including but not limited to** the Data Access guidelines available at `https://www.cmu.edu/data/guidelines/data-access.html`
- any additional instructions provided by Girls of Steel leadership for specific systems, events, or situations

If there is a conflict between this agreement and a controlling Carnegie Mellon University policy, legal requirement, or institutional directive, the stricter or controlling requirement applies.

## 15. Acknowledgment of consequences

I understand that failure to follow this agreement may result in action including:

- removal of access
- restriction of mentoring duties
- removal from events or programs
- termination of volunteer or mentor status
- referral to the university or other appropriate authority, if applicable

## 16. Mentor acknowledgment

By accepting this agreement, I confirm that:

- I have read and understood this Mentor Data Access and Safeguarding Agreement
- I will use Girls of Steel data only for authorized program purposes
- I will protect the confidentiality of student, family, alumni, and program information
- I will report suspected or actual data issues immediately
- I understand that my use of program systems may be logged and reviewed
- I understand that my access is conditioned on compliance with this agreement and other applicable policies
